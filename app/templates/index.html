Project roadmap

Building RansomShield one validated layer at a time.

Status reflects local implementation and testing as of 14 October 2026. Synthetic results do not establish real-world detection performance.

Current focusCompatible published telemetry, physical two-laptop transfer, and real notification delivery verification.
Phase 01Completed

Research and system design

Define the problem, related work, behavioral signals, and hybrid architecture.

02
Phase 02Completed

Flask application and accounts

Authentication, live dashboard, run history, incident details and downloadable CSV reports are implemented and locally tested.

03
Phase 03Completed

Synthetic model and safe response

TCN + XGBoost trained on 6,000 synthetic sequences. Safe simulations, telemetry CSV evaluation, test-file quarantine and configurable notifications are locally tested.

04
Phase 04In progress

Published dataset integration

Published MLRan CSV integrated with separately trained XGBoost: 2,300 training and 324 held-out samples. Temporal TCN validation on published data remains pending.

05
Phase 05Planned

External validation and two-laptop delivery

Verify secure transfer on actual laptops, real email/webhook delivery and independent published-data performance.
